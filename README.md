## Project overview

*Disaster-Ready Doon* is a research-based practical guide examining earthquake readiness, landslide exposure, forest-fire preparedness, early-warning information, and community resilience in the Doon Valley.

The work translates a review of studies, official material, and reporting into practical checklists and planning prompts for households, schools, hotels, and neighbourhoods. It is shared here as a **research-work sample and documentation of the project**, not as an official government publication or an independently certified risk assessment.

## Research areas

- **Doon landscape and hazards** — the relationship between terrain, exposure, vulnerability, and capacity.
- **Earthquake readiness** — building-safety considerations, household walk-throughs, and protective actions.
- **Landslides** — the Dehradun–Mussoorie corridor, susceptibility mapping, warning signs, and travel decisions.
- **Forest fires** — seasonal context, interpreting alerts, and household preparedness near forest edges.
- **Warnings and misinformation** — distinguishing monitoring, early warning, forecasting, and prediction.
- **Preparedness and resilience** — family plans, emergency kits, institutional drills, and neighbourhood action plans.

## Research integrity and limitations

The manuscript distinguishes established findings, reported information, proposals, and items marked `VERIFY`. Some source entries and emergency-contact details are explicitly marked for further verification in the manuscript. Readers should consult the original source and current official guidance before relying on safety-critical details.

This document is general educational guidance. It is not an official emergency instruction, engineering assessment, legal opinion, or medical advice. Maps and diagrams are schematic, not site-specific risk assessments. Follow current instructions from authorised emergency responders.
